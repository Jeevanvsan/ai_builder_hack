She can't dial 112. He's sitting right next to her. But she can order dinner.

For the AI Builder Cup 2026 we built QuickOne: an everyday ordering app for food, groceries, medicines and home repairs, where every way of ordering is a hidden line to a response team.

In the caller's hand, it's just an ordering app. Call to order and you reach Mia, a Gemini Live voice agent who teaches you the code while she takes your order: "garlic bread means someone is following you." The person in the room hears dinner.

On the responder's console, the siren rings before the AI has said a word. Within seconds it's a live, located emergency: the threat, the people involved, voice stress, what the camera saw, and a route to the nearest police station.

Under the hood, Gemini Live turns every answer into a function call, Cloud Firestore streams it to the console in real time, rules in our code (not the model) decide severity, and Google Maps Platform finds the caller from a landmark and the way to safety.
