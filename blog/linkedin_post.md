She can't dial 112. He's sitting right next to her. But she can order dinner.

## Jeevan (the responder's side)

The siren rang before the AI had said a word.

That's the first thing a responder sees in QuickOne, the app Team NexMind built for the AI Builder Cup 2026. In the caller's hand it looks like an everyday ordering app, and to anyone watching it's someone ordering dinner. On our console it's a live, located emergency: the threat, the people involved, voice stress, what the camera saw, and a route to the nearest police station.

Under the hood, Gemini Live turns every answer into a function call, Cloud Firestore streams it to the console in real time, rules in our code (not the model) decide severity, and Google Maps Platform finds the caller and the way to safety.
