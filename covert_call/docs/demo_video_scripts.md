# QuickBite demo video scripts

Two scenes for the demo video:
- **Scene A:** covert call (an attacker is in the room)
- **Scene B:** chase call (someone is following the caller)

Layout for both: phone on the left, dashboard on the right, subtitles in the top bar, callouts in the space under the dashboard.

**Who says what:**
- **Caller:** played by ChatGPT voice. Paste the setup prompt below into ChatGPT first.
- **Mia:** the real QuickBite AI (Gemini Live). Her lines below are what she is expected to say. She improvises, so the wording will vary. Don't correct her mid-call, just keep the caller on script.
- **Callout:** on-screen text added in editing, timed to when it appears on the dashboard.

Record each call in full, then cut the pauses in editing. Mark any time skip with a small "Later" label, so the video doesn't look faked.

---

## ChatGPT setup prompts (paste before each take)

**Scene A:**
> You are role-playing a caller in a short safety-app demo. You are a woman at home. Your husband is in the room, angry, holding a hammer. You're pretending to order food so he doesn't realise you're asking for help. Speak quietly and nervously, in short answers of 1 to 6 words. Wait for the other person to finish before you answer. Only say the lines I give you, in order, one per turn, as the other person asks. Lines: [paste the CALLER lines from Scene A]

**Scene B:**
> You are role-playing a caller in a short safety-app demo. You are a woman walking home at night. A man on a dark motorbike has been following you. You speak plainly, out of breath and scared, in short sentences. Wait for the other person to finish before you answer. Only say the lines I give you, in order, one per turn. Lines: [paste the CALLER lines from Scene B]

If ChatGPT goes off script, it's fine for the caller to read the lines themselves. The live AI is Mia, not the caller.

## Scene B: Chase call (being followed) ~1:35–2:15

**Setup:** walk (or play it walking) on a real street with phone GPS on and precise location allowed. The dashboard map shows the live track and route.

| # | Speaker | Line | Dashboard / callout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mia | "Hi, thanks for calling QuickBite, this is Mia. Can you talk freely, or shall we keep it like a normal order?" | New incident appears. |
| 2 | Caller | *(breathless)* "Talk. Someone is following me." | Tag: *being followed or chased*. Severity HIGH. |
| 3 | Mia | "Okay, I'm here with you. Are you walking, or in a vehicle?" | |
| 4 | Caller | "Walking. He's on a bike behind me." | **Callout:** "Mia switches to plain talk: the caller isn't hiding" |
| 5 | Mia | "Keep walking towards the main road. The nearest police station is about [distance] away. At the next junction, turn [left/right]." | Route line to the police station draws on the map, with ETA. **Callout:** "Route to the nearest police station, from live GPS" |
| 6 | Caller | "Okay. I'm at a junction now." | The position dot moves along the track. |
| 7 | Mia | "Good. Turn [direction] and keep going straight about [distance]. Stay where there are lights and people." | Next step updates on the route tile. |
| 8 | Mia | "What colour is the bike: dark, white, or bright?" | |
| 9 | Caller | "Dark. Black bike." | Tag: vehicle details. **Callout:** "Details for the patrol, gathered between turns" |
| 10 | Caller | *(later)* "I can see the police station." | **"Later" label.** The dot reaches the destination. |
| 11 | Mia | "Go straight in and tell them someone followed you. You did really well. Are you safe now?" | |
| 12 | Caller | "Yes, I'm inside." | Call ends → summary + bulletin. |

The bracketed parts are what Mia reads from the real route, so don't script them. For a reliable take, rehearse the walk once on the same street first.

## Before each take
- Clear old test incidents from the Live queue so the new one is obvious (resolve them).
- Turn off notifications on both machines. Keep the browser full screen with no tabs showing, and the phone fully charged.
- Place the speaker playing ChatGPT near the phone mic. Do a 20-second test and check that the Conversation tab fills in.
- Start both recordings, then clap once near both machines to give a sync point.
