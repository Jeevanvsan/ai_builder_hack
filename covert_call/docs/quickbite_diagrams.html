QuickBite — the disguise, three screens

An ordinary food-delivery app on the surface. The two modes fork from the home screen and never touch each other — one leads to a live disguised call, the other to a silent tap-only checkout. Reload the tab to replay the entrance animation.

Monitoring Dashboard — a separate app, built for a response team

Own deployment, own URL, designed for a wall monitor — not a phone-sized view of QuickBite. Five pages: the live queue, live video (stretch goal), the raw notification feed, the Gemini-written call-end report, and resolved-case history. Product decision: this dashboard is light-themed, shown here pinned to light regardless of your system theme, to match the actual planned UI (see quickbite_plan.md) — not a dark ops-console look.
