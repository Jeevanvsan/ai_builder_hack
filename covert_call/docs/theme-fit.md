# Theme fit — "Sustainability & Social Impact"

*The team's settled answer to "why does QuickBite fit this theme?" — Problem Alignment & Impact is 25% of scoring,
so this needs to be decided before the deck, not improvised in Q&A. Grounded in the theme's own wording, not an
invented environmental angle.*

## The theme's exact wording
> "Build an AI-powered solution that addresses a meaningful environmental or **societal** challenge."
> Named approaches: resource efficiency, accessibility, **resilience strengthening**, **community support**,
> informed decision-making.

The theme is "environmental **or** societal." QuickBite is a **societal**-impact project. We do **not** claim an
environmental angle — that would be a stretch judges would see through. We anchor on two of the theme's own named
approaches: **resilience strengthening** and **community support**.

## Our answer (say it in one breath)
> "QuickBite strengthens the resilience of people who are in danger but can't safely ask for help — someone being
> coerced, watched, or held — by turning an ordinary-looking phone into a covert channel to a response team. It's
> community support in its most literal form: getting help to a person when speaking up openly would put them at
> greater risk."

## Why it's an honest fit (three points for the deck)
1. **Resilience strengthening** = helping vulnerable people withstand and get through a crisis. QuickBite exists for
   the moment when the normal path (call 911, shout for help, open a safety app) is exactly what a person *can't*
   do because they're being watched. It adds a layer of resilience where none existed.
2. **Community support** = connecting people who need help to those who can give it. QuickBite is the bridge:
   disguised request in, coordinated response out, with a real responder console.
3. **AI is the core, not a wrapper.** The impact is only possible *because* Gemini Live can hold a natural,
   adaptive, disguised conversation and extract structured incident data live — a scripted bot or a form couldn't
   do this without breaking the disguise. (This is also the Technical Merit story, 40%.)

## What we will NOT claim (honesty guardrails)
- No environmental/sustainability-of-resources claim. It's societal impact, full stop.
- We don't claim to replace emergency services or guarantee a real-world dispatch — the "response" in this
  prototype is the dashboard incident, and we say so.
- We don't overstate novelty of individual pieces (mesh, anonymisation, crash detection all exist elsewhere); the
  novel part is the disguised-live-teaching-call + real-time extraction combination (see plan §8).

## One-line version for a slide
> **Societal impact — resilience & community support: an AI that lets a person in danger ask for help when asking
> out loud isn't safe.**
