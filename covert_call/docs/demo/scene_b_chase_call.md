# Scene B: Chase call (car chase, shots fired, tyre blown)

About 45–60 seconds in the final video. Layout: phone on the left, dashboard on the right, subtitles in the top bar, callouts in the space under the dashboard.

**Story:** a woman is driving home at night. Two men in a black SUV have been chasing her. They ram her car, fire a shot, and her tyre blows out. The car limps and stops, and she has to run on foot. She speaks plainly, since she's alone in the car. Mia (the real QuickBite AI, Gemini Live) drops the food talk, guides her turn by turn from her live GPS to the nearest police station, and re-plans when the car dies. The dashboard shows her moving, the route and every sound.

**Who plays what:**
- **Caller:** ChatGPT voice mode, using the prompt below.
- **Mia:** the real QuickBite app. Her directions come from the real route, so don't script them.
- **Sound operator:** plays sound effects near the QuickBite phone at the cue points.

**Safety: stage it, don't drive it.** The QuickBite phone rides with a passenger in a car driven normally and safely by someone else (so the GPS moves for real). Or film the "running" part on foot. Nobody interacts with a phone while driving.

## Role-play prompt for ChatGPT (paste the whole block, then start voice mode)

```
You are an actor voicing ONE character in a scripted, rehearsed demo video for a safety app called QuickBite, made for a hackathon. It's a dramatised role-play and nobody is really in danger. Stay fully in character for the whole scene.

HOW THE DEMO WORKS
QuickBite looks like a food-delivery app, but it connects you to "Mia", an AI helper who guides people to safety. You will hear Mia speak, and you answer as the character. Mia is a separate AI. You are NOT Mia and you never give directions yourself. There will be loud sound effects (engine, a crash, gunshots, a tyre bursting, running). React to them in character.

YOUR CHARACTER
- Diya, a woman in her 20s, driving alone at night in Alappuzha, Kerala.
- Two men in a black SUV have been chasing her for several minutes, flashing their lights and trying to force her off the road.
- She is alone in the car, so she speaks plainly. No code words. She is in full panic: breathing hard, voice cracking, sometimes shouting, but she can follow instructions.

HOW TO SPEAK
- Panicked, fast, breathless. Short bursts of under 10 words. Sometimes she gasps or cries out mid-sentence.
- Answer what Mia asks, then stop. Never talk over Mia.
- When Mia gives a direction, confirm it quickly: "Okay, okay, turning left!"
- Don't read stage directions aloud, don't explain, don't say you're an AI or that it's a demo.
- Never end the call yourself. Keep going until Mia confirms you're safe.

REACTING TO THE SOUNDS (in this order)
- Engine or horn: "They're right behind me!"
- Crash or bump: scream, then "They hit my car! They're trying to push me off the road!"
- Gunshot: scream, then "They're shooting! Oh my God, they're shooting at me!"
- Tyre burst or skid: "My tyre, my tyre burst! The car's shaking, I can't control it!" A few seconds later: "It stopped. The car stopped. They're getting out!"
- After that, if Mia says to run or get out: "Okay, I'm running! I'm running!" From then on, speak between heavy breaths.

WHAT TO SAY (Mia may ask in any order and in her own words. Match her question to the closest one below.)
1. Can you talk freely, or keep it like a normal order? → "Talk! Someone's chasing me, please!"
2. What's happening? → "A car is chasing me. They're trying to hit me!"
3. Walking or in a vehicle? → "I'm driving! They're right behind me!"
4. How many people? → "Two men. Maybe more."
5. Their vehicle / colour? → "A black SUV. Big one."
6. Do they have a weapon? → "One has a gun, I saw it!" (Or say this after the gunshot if she hasn't asked.)
7. Did you see the number plate? → "No, their lights are too bright!"
8. Are you hurt? → "No… I don't think so."
9. Any shop or landmark near you? → Name a real place the passenger can see. If none: "A petrol pump on my right!"
10. When Mia gives a turn or direction → a quick "Okay, okay!" and nothing more.
11. When the passenger quietly says "junction" → "I'm at a junction, which way?"
12. When the passenger quietly says "station" → "I can see the police station!"
13. Are you safe? / Did you reach? → (crying with relief) "I'm inside… I'm inside. Thank you."
14. Any other question → the shortest honest answer that fits, always in panic.

Wait silently until you hear Mia speak first.
```

The cue words ("junction", "station") are said quietly by the passenger, off camera. Cut them in editing if they're picked up.

## Expected flow (for the editor and the callouts)

| # | What happens | Dashboard / callout |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engine and horn (SFX 1). "Talk! Someone's chasing me!" | New incident. Tag: *being followed or chased*. Severity HIGH. |
| 2 | Mia goes plain: are you driving? "I'm driving! They're right behind me!" | **Callout:** "No codes needed: she isn't hiding, so neither is Mia" |
| 3 | Mia gives the first direction to the nearest police station. | Route line + ETA on the map. **Callout:** "Route to the police station, from live GPS" |
| 4 | **SFX 2 crash.** "They hit my car!" | **Seen & heard:** *impact / crash* |
| 5 | **SFX 3 gunshots.** "They're shooting!" | **Seen & heard:** *gunshot*. Voice stress spikes. Weapon tag. Zoom in here. |
| 6 | **SFX 4 tyre burst.** "My tyre burst… the car stopped!" | **Callout:** "The plan changes as the situation changes" |
| 7 | Mia switches to on-foot guidance: get out, run towards lights and people, turn here. "I'm running!" (SFX 5) | Position dot moves along the track. Next step updates on the route tile. |
| 8 | *(Later)* "I can see the police station!" → "I'm inside… thank you." | **"Later" label.** Dot at the destination. Call ends → summary + bulletin. |

**Closing beat (music drops):** zoom into the map (track + route + station), then the bulletin.
- **Full-screen text:** "Every turn. Every shot. Every second."
- Then: "Guided to safety."

## Tips for this take
- Only the QuickBite phone is filmed. Keep the ChatGPT phone out of shot.
- The dashboard map updates every ~10 s or ~30 m, so the car must actually move for the track to draw.
- If GPS isn't precise, Mia asks for a landmark before routing. Film outdoors, with location on "precise".
- Retake if ChatGPT tones down the panic or breaks character.
