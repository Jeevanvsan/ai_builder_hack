# QuickBite — 3-minute demo video script (Epic 5.2)

*Target: under 3:00. The single strongest beat is the split-screen live update — disguised call on one device,
dashboard populating live on another. Build the whole video around that moment. Rehearse the persona flow
end-to-end first (Epic 5.2) so a call never misreads the disguise on camera.*

## Setup for filming
- **Device A (phone/left):** the QuickBite web app (or native), `https://quickbite-5cde0.web.app`.
- **Device B (right):** the Monitoring Dashboard, `https://quickbite-5cde0-dashboard.web.app`, signed in, on the
  live queue.
- Split-screen or picture-in-picture so both are visible during the call.
- Have a plausible "delivery address" ready to say aloud.

## Beat sheet

**0:00–0:20 — The problem (hook).**
Voiceover over the QuickBite home screen: "When someone is being watched or coerced, the one thing they can't do is
openly ask for help. QuickBite looks like an ordinary food-delivery app — because it has to." Scroll the menu; it
reads as completely normal.

**0:20–0:35 — The disguise holds.**
Show the two ordinary paths: browse menu, tap "Call to order". Make the point on screen: nothing here looks like a
safety app.

**0:35–1:30 — The live call (the core).**
Place the call. Mia answers as a restaurant employee. Show 2–3 coded exchanges, and each time flash a caption of the
REAL meaning being spoken in the same breath (e.g. "extra pepperoni = a weapon is present"). **Cut to the dashboard
(Device B) filling in live** — new incident appears at call start, then people count, danger indicators, location,
voice-stress meter populate field-by-field *while the call is still going*. This is the money shot: keep both sides
on screen.

**1:30–1:55 — The AI is really reasoning.**
Call out, briefly: it sees the back camera (a scene observation appears), it hears the room, it escalates severity
on a danger signal, and it gives the caller a piece of disguised safety advice. One sentence each, over the
dashboard updating.

**1:55–2:20 — The other ways in.**
Quick montage: (1) the silent tap screen (no sound needed) + a photo attachment that Gemini reads; (2) the coded
"click & order" checkout that raises an incident and shows a normal "order placed" screen; (3) the heart
double-tap silent SOS going to a black "phone-off" screen while the dashboard shows a live SOS with front+back
video. Fast — 6–8 seconds each.

**2:20–2:45 — The responder side.**
On the dashboard: acknowledge the incident, show the consolidated case summary written after the call, the map, the
Drive video link. "One shared AI backbone: disguised request in, coordinated response out."

**2:45–3:00 — Close.**
Theme line (see theme-fit.md): "Societal impact — resilience and community support: an AI that lets a person in
danger ask for help when asking out loud isn't safe." End card: app + dashboard URLs, "Built on Gemini + Firebase."

## Rehearsal checklist (before recording)
- [ ] Run the full call once; confirm the persona never processes it as a literal food order.
- [ ] Confirm the dashboard updates live during the call (not only at the end).
- [ ] Confirm the coded-order and silent-SOS paths each create an incident.
- [ ] Confirm the live video connects (test on the actual devices/network you'll film on).
- [ ] Have a fallback recording of the split-screen moment in case the live network is flaky on the day.
